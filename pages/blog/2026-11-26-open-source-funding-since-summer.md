Hello everyone,

This summer, I told you that [wallabag.it was funding development work for wallabag](/en/blog/2026/06/30/wallabag-it-funds-open-source-development/), and [the first contributions arrived in July](/en/blog/2026/07/23/optional-captcha-wallabag-wallabag-it-funding/).

It kept going since then, still with [Yassine Guedidi](https://github.com/yguedidi). Here’s what was added to wallabag:

- [passwords that already appear in data leaks](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/8767) can be refused at sign-up or when you change your password (option disabled by default);
- people who host their own instance can [stop wallabag from contacting some websites](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/8966);
- [the buttons of the interface](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/8978) work better with a keyboard and a screen reader;
- [the menus were reorganised behind the scenes](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/9192): nothing changes for you, but they will be easier to improve;
- and a few fixes, for [the Tags menu](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/9180), [export links](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/9183), [configuration](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/9175), [installation](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/8976) and [the development environment](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/pull/8975).

All of this will be in the next wallabag release, 2.7.

In 2026, **wallabag.it invested almost €3,000 in the open source project**. That’s also what your subscriptions are for: not only paying for hosting and support, but also work that helps everyone who uses wallabag.

Thanks to all subscribers 🦘 ❤️

See you soon,

Nicolas

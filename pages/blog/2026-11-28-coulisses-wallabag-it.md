Hello everyone,

Lately, I've been working on things you can't see, but that make running wallabag.it a lot easier for me. And indirectly, they improve your day-to-day use of the service:

- when a major error happens, I get notified right away. I've already fixed several bugs this way, for example imports that got stuck or exports that failed when there were too many articles.
- all your messages to the support now arrive in the same place, and you can write to me from the app.
- invoices for payments made since June 2026 can be downloaded from your account. For older ones, just send me a quick email.
- I test every new version on a copy of the service before putting it online, and I can easily roll back if something goes wrong. So I can release new versions more easily (and with more peace of mind).

These changes save me time, and that's time I can spend on the project.

Thank you for your support 🦘 ❤️

Have a nice day,
Nicolas

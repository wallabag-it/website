wallabag.it is a paid service built on free software. Your subscriptions don’t only pay for servers and support: they also help the project.

Since July 2026, wallabag.it has directly funded development work, done by [Yassine Guedidi](https://github.com/yguedidi) and by me, Nicolas. **By December 31, 2026, wallabag.it will have invested almost €4,000 in the open source project.**

For the details, read [the announcement](/en/blog/2026/06/30/wallabag-it-funds-open-source-development/), [the first contributions](/en/blog/2026/07/23/optional-captcha-wallabag-wallabag-it-funding/) and [the November 2026 update](/en/blog/2026/11/24/your-subscriptions-keep-making-wallabag-better/).

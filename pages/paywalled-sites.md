## How it works

1. In wallabag.it, open **Site credentials** in the menu.
2. Add the site, for example `lemonde.fr`, with the login and password of your subscription.
3. Save articles from this site as usual: wallabag.it logs in for you and keeps the full text.

Your login is stored encrypted and only used to fetch the articles you save. wallabag.it doesn’t get around paywalls: you need your own subscription.

Websites change their login page from time to time. If an article from one of these sites comes back incomplete, [let us know](mailto:helpdesk@wallabag.it) and we’ll fix it.

## Obsidian Web Clipper

Obsidian Web Clipper is free and open source. It's a browser extension that saves web pages, highlights and metadata as Markdown notes in your Obsidian vault.

If you already work in Obsidian, it's a very good tool: templates, local files, links between notes.

But it's made for Obsidian. It's a way to take notes, not a reading list.

## Two different habits

- With **Obsidian Web Clipper**, you keep a page as a note, to work with it later.
- With **wallabag.it**, you save an article to read it later in a clean view, on the web, on your phone or on your e-reader.

You can use both: wallabag.it to read, Obsidian for the notes you really want to keep.

## What wallabag.it does

- It saves the whole article, not just the link. If the page disappears, you still have it.
- You read on the web, on Android, on iOS, and on Kobo or PocketBook e-readers. Offline too.
- Tags, search, highlights and annotations.
- You can export everything whenever you want: PDF, ePUB, mobi, JSON, CSV…
- It's hosted in Europe. No ads, no tracking: it's paid for by subscriptions.
- It's the open-source wallabag, the project I started in 2013. If you'd rather host it yourself, you can.

## Omnivore has shut down

Omnivore was a nice open-source read-it-later app, with highlights and Obsidian or Logseq integrations. In late 2024 its team joined ElevenLabs and the hosted service closed.

If you liked Omnivore because it was open source, wallabag is the same kind of project. The difference is that wallabag.it is paid, and that's also why it's still here.

## The other options

- **Readwise Reader**: very complete (highlights, notes, RSS, AI features), but around 96 € a year, proprietary and hosted in the US.
- **Instapaper**: a good reading app. The best features need Premium, around 55 € a year.
- **Raindrop.io**: a bookmark manager more than a reading app.

## Moving from Omnivore

wallabag.it imports your Omnivore export, in one go. Imports are part of the subscription, not of the free trial.

## What wallabag.it does

- It saves the whole article, not just the link. If the page disappears, you still have it.
- You read on the web, on Android, on iOS, and on Kobo or PocketBook e-readers. Offline too.
- Tags, search, highlights and annotations.
- You can export everything whenever you want: PDF, ePUB, mobi, JSON, CSV…
- It's hosted in Europe. No ads, no tracking: it's paid for by subscriptions.
- It's the open-source wallabag, the project I started in 2013. If you'd rather host it yourself, you can.

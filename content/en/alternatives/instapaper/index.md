## Instapaper

Instapaper has been around since 2008, it's one of the first read-it-later apps. The reading view is nice and lots of people like it.

But searching your own articles, unlimited highlights and text-to-speech need Premium, around 55 € a year. The app also changed hands a few times: Pinterest bought it in 2016, then sold it in 2018.

## The other options

- **Readwise Reader**: very complete (RSS, highlights, notes), but around 96 € a year.
- **Matter**: nicely designed, made for the iPhone first.

## Moving from Instapaper

wallabag.it imports your Instapaper articles. [Here's the step-by-step guide](https://helpdesk.wallabag.it/help/en-gb/2-import/11-how-to-migrate-from-instapaper-to-wallabag-it). Imports are part of the subscription, not of the free trial.

## What wallabag.it does

- It saves the whole article, not just the link. If the page disappears, you still have it.
- You read on the web, on Android, on iOS, and on Kobo or PocketBook e-readers. Offline too.
- Tags, search, highlights and annotations.
- You can export everything whenever you want: PDF, ePUB, mobi, JSON, CSV…
- It's hosted in Europe. No ads, no tracking: it's paid for by subscriptions.
- It's the open-source wallabag, the project I started in 2013. If you'd rather host it yourself, you can.

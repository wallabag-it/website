## Pocket has shut down

Mozilla shut Pocket down in 2025. If you had years of articles in there, you're probably looking for a new home for them.

## The other options

- **Instapaper**: a nice reading app, with highlights and text-to-speech. Search and the best features need Premium, around 55 € a year.
- **Raindrop.io**: a bookmark manager. It has a reader view, but it's made to organise links, not to read them.
- **Pinboard**: simple and reliable bookmarking, but no reading view and no offline reading.

## Moving from Pocket

wallabag.it imports your Pocket export, with your tags and read/favourite status. [Here's the step-by-step guide](https://helpdesk.wallabag.it/help/en-gb/2-import/6-how-to-migrate-from-pocket-to-wallabag-it). Imports are part of the subscription, not of the free trial.

## What wallabag.it does

- It saves the whole article, not just the link. If the page disappears, you still have it.
- You read on the web, on Android, on iOS, and on Kobo or PocketBook e-readers. Offline too.
- Tags, search, highlights and annotations.
- You can export everything whenever you want: PDF, ePUB, mobi, JSON, CSV…
- It's hosted in Europe. No ads, no tracking: it's paid for by subscriptions.
- It's the open-source wallabag, the project I started in 2013. If you'd rather host it yourself, you can.

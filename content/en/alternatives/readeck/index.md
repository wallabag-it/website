## Readeck

Readeck is one of the nicest read-it-later projects of the last few years. It's open source, modern, and made to be self-hosted.

If you like running your own server (backups, updates, monitoring), it's a good option.

## Self-hosted or hosted?

The choice is mostly about who maintains the server:

- **self-hosting**: you control everything, and you do the maintenance;
- **a hosted service**: you pay a small subscription and someone else keeps it running.

wallabag.it is the second one. And since wallabag is open source too, you can host it yourself one day if you want.

## What wallabag.it does

- It saves the whole article, not just the link. If the page disappears, you still have it.
- You read on the web, on Android, on iOS, and on Kobo or PocketBook e-readers. Offline too.
- Tags, search, highlights and annotations.
- You can export everything whenever you want: PDF, ePUB, mobi, JSON, CSV…
- It's hosted in Europe. No ads, no tracking: it's paid for by subscriptions.
- It's the open-source wallabag, the project I started in 2013. If you'd rather host it yourself, you can.

## What a subscription adds
{.text-center .mb-5}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fa-envelope" title="Save by email" %}}
Get your private address `save+USER+TOKEN@wallabag.it`: send or forward an email containing links and they are saved automatically. Handy for the links your friends and family send you.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fa-cloud-download" title="Import from another service" %}}
Bring your reading list from Pocket, Instapaper, Pinboard, Omnivore, Shaarli or another wallabag. Tags and read/favourite status are kept.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

{{% blocks/feature icon="fa-rss" title="Follow RSS & Atom feeds" %}}
Paste the address of a website: wallabag.it finds its feed and saves the new articles in your account, automatically if you want.
{{% /blocks/feature %}}

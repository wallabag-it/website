## Organizer

The draw is organized by Nicolas Lœuillet, sole proprietor (entrepreneur individuel), publisher of wallabag.it, SIRET 823 678 560 00025, 30 bis rue des sapins, 62720 Rinxent, France. Contact: [nicolas@wallabag.it](mailto:nicolas@wallabag.it).

## Draw date

The draw takes place on December 11, 2026, Paris time.

## Who takes part

All wallabag.it accounts active on the day of the draw, that is, with a subscription running on December 11, 2026, on any plan and whatever the date it was taken out.

The draw is open in all countries, except those whose law forbids taking part.

There's nothing to do to take part. Each account has one chance. The free trial doesn't count, and the organizer can't win.

## Prize

There is one prize, for one person: 10 years of subscription added to the winning account's subscription, on the same plan. Its value is at least €110 (10 years at €11). The prize can't be transferred or exchanged for money.

## Draw

The winning account is picked at random by a computer program, among the eligible accounts. The organizer keeps the list of participating accounts for 6 months after the draw, then deletes it, along with the result.

## How the winner is told

The winner is contacted by email, at the address of their wallabag.it account. Without a reply within 30 days, the prize goes back into a new draw.

## Withdrawal and refund

The 14-day right of withdrawal set out in the [terms](/en/terms/) applies as usual to all participants. A subscription withdrawn from or refunded before the draw doesn't take part.

By accepting the prize, the winner waives the right to withdraw from, or ask for a refund of, the subscription that made them eligible. If they prefer to withdraw or be refunded, they don't receive the prize, which goes back into a new draw.

## Announcement of the result

The result is announced in a post on the wallabag.it blog. The winner's name only appears with their agreement.

## Personal data

The draw only uses data already linked to the account (email address, payments). It is processed to run the draw and hand over the prize, and the list of participants is deleted 6 months after the draw. Your rights are described in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).

## Applicable law

These rules are governed by French law, without depriving participants of the protections given by the law of their country. For any question or complaint: [nicolas@wallabag.it](mailto:nicolas@wallabag.it).

Pocket has shut down. If you had a reading list there, you can bring it to wallabag.it. It takes about ten minutes.

## Step 1: export your Pocket data

1. Go to [getpocket.com/export](https://getpocket.com/export)
2. Download the export file, a CSV with all your saved URLs

If you can no longer access your Pocket account, check whether Mozilla sent you an export email.

## Step 2: create a wallabag.it account

It's [right here](https://app.wallabag.it/register/): 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

## Step 3: import your data

1. In your account, open **Import** in the left menu
2. Select **Pocket**
3. Upload the CSV file from step 1

wallabag.it fetches the content of each article. Your articles show up in your reading list within a few minutes, and your Pocket tags are kept.

## Step 4: install the apps

- **Browser**: the [Firefox extension](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/wallabag-v2/) or the [Chrome extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/wallabagger/gbmgphmejlcoihgedabhgjdpkiiiljog)
- **Android and iOS**: the mobile app, to read offline
- **Kobo e-reader**: wallabag.it connects directly to Kobo devices

To compare with other services, I also wrote [a page about Pocket alternatives](/en/alternatives/pocket/).

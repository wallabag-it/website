Hello everyone,

wallabag.it opened in 2016. Ten years later, the website and the app were showing their age. I wanted a change, so both of them now have a new design.

And since you don't turn 10 every day, there's a surprise at the end of this post 🎁

{{< screenshot name="homepage.png" alt="The wallabag.it reading list with the new design" >}}

## In the app

- a sidebar menu, always visible on a computer, with your article counts
- a calmer reading page: the "Aa" button lets you choose the font, the text size, the line width and the theme (light, sepia or dark)
- easier to read annotations
- the login, sign-up, tags and configuration pages have been reworked, in light and dark mode
- a simpler subscription page

Nothing changes for your articles, your tags and your settings. The mobile apps and the browser extensions work as before, and so do the keyboard shortcuts.

## On the website

The website uses the same colors as the app. The texts of the home page have been slightly reworked, and the rest seems easier to read to me.

Another new thing: [a demo of the app](/demo/), to see what it looks like without creating an account. There are about thirty articles in it: you can browse the list, open an article, change the font or switch to sepia. It's a frozen copy of the app, so you can't save anything, and it's only available in English.

## What about the open source project?

Since last year, [a discussion has been open on the wallabag project](https://github.com/wallabag/wallabag/discussions/8467) to redesign its interface, with lots of ideas and mockups. The new design of wallabag.it is heavily inspired by it: thanks to [Timothée Julien, independent product designer](https://timotheejulien.fr/), for his excellent work.

wallabag.it is wallabag with a few extra features. And this new design was made without touching the code of the open source project: it sits on top of it. So I can test it for real, with you, on a service used every day, before suggesting what works to the open source project. A bit like [the development work funded by wallabag.it](/en/blog/2026/06/30/wallabag-it-funds-open-source-development/): what you pay also helps move wallabag forward for everyone.

So your feedback counts twice. If something doesn't look right for you, if a button is missing, or if you preferred the old layout for a specific reason, let me know: with the "Your feedback" button in the app menu, or at nicolas@wallabag.it.

It's summer!

Even if you don't go far, the pace changes a little and you sometimes find a few moments for yourself. That is often when you remember that list of articles saved "for later" (who said "to never read them"?).

A long read spotted between two tasks, an investigation too long to read on the spot, a technical post you really wanted to understand... Over time, the list grows.

No need to read everything. One or two articles on the train, on a bench, at the beach, or in the evening when the day winds down, that's already good.

With wallabag.it, they follow you on your computer, phone, tablet, and even your e-reader depending on your setup. You can read them offline and find them with tags or search.

And if you come across articles you no longer care about, delete them. That feels good too.

If you want to get back to it, the yearly subscription starts at 11 euros, less than one euro a month. It helps keep the service and the open-source project going.

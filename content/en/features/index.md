## Android and iOS apps for offline reading

There's an app for your phone. It downloads your unread articles, and you can read them without a connection: on the metro, on a plane, or in the countryside.

- **Android**: the app syncs your account and downloads your unread articles.
- **iPhone and iPad**: your articles are there, even offline.
- **E-readers**: Kobo and PocketBook, through community apps.
